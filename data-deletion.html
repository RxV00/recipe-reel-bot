Data Deletion Instructions

You can permanently delete everything Recipe Reel Bot has stored about your account at any time, directly from Instagram — no separate account or web form required.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Instagram and go to your DM thread with Recipe Reel Bot.
  2. Send the bot a message containing the single word DELETE.
  3. The bot erases all stored data associated with your account.
This is permanent

Sending DELETE cannot be undone. It removes your saved cookbook (every recipe you've extracted), your shopping list, and your account record — the IGSID, message IDs, and any other data tied to you. There is no way to recover this data afterward, so only send DELETE if you're sure.

What this does not affect

Deleting your data does not affect the original Instagram posts you shared, the creators who made them, or anyone else's cookbook. It only removes what Recipe Reel Bot itself has stored about your account.

Media that's already gone

In the normal course of using the bot, any downloaded video, audio, and extracted frames from a shared post are already deleted within 24 hours of processing, well before you'd ever need to request deletion — see the Privacy Policy for details. Sending DELETE additionally removes the recipes and account data that are kept longer, until you ask for them to be removed.

Questions

This is a personal project without a support team. If deletion doesn't seem to have worked, or you have a question, message the operator directly via Instagram DM.
