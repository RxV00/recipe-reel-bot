Privacy Policy

Recipe Reel Bot is an early-access, personal project. This page explains what data it collects, why, who it's shared with, and how long it's kept — in plain language, not legalese.

What we collect

When you share a Reel or post with the bot over Instagram DM, we collect:

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID (IGSID) — an identifier tied to your Instagram account for this app, not your Instagram password or profile.
  • Message IDs for the DMs you send the bot.
  • Metadata about the media you share (e.g. the post's permalink, caption, and creator information).

To build a recipe, the caption text and, when the shared post's video is downloadable, its audio track and a small number of sampled video frames are processed. These are read once to extract a recipe; the bot does not scrape Instagram or access content you haven't shared with it.

Who we share it with

To turn a Reel into a structured recipe, captions, audio transcripts, and sampled frames are sent to third-party AI providers for processing:

  • OpenAI — audio transcription and reasoning (turning evidence into a structured recipe).
  • Google — vision analysis of sampled video frames (e.g. reading on-screen text).
  • Anthropic — reasoning, used for harder extraction cases.

These providers process the data to return a result to the bot; they are not used to build a public profile of you, and the bot doesn't sell your data to anyone.

How long we keep it

Downloaded video, audio, and extracted frames are cached only for as long as it takes to process them, and are deleted within 24 hours regardless of outcome. The derived recipe — the ingredients, steps, timing, and nutrition the AI reconstructed — stays in your personal cookbook until you delete it yourself.

Creator attribution

Every recipe saved through the bot is derived from someone else's Instagram post. We keep and display that creator's username and a link to their original post alongside the recipe, as attribution. We never redistribute or rehost the original video.

Accuracy disclaimer

Extracted recipes and nutrition figures are automated estimates, not dietary, nutritional, or medical advice. Verify anything that matters before you rely on it — allergens especially.

Your choices

You can ask us to permanently delete everything we hold about your account at any time — see the Data Deletion page for the exact steps.

Contact

This is a personal project without a support team. If you have a question about your data, message the operator directly via Instagram DM.

Changes to this policy

Because this is an early-access project, this policy may change as the product evolves. The "last updated" date in the footer of every page reflects the most recent revision.
