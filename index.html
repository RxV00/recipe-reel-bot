Send us a Reel. Get back a recipe.

Share any Instagram Reel with Recipe Reel Bot's DM inbox and it reads the caption, and where available the audio track and a handful of sampled video frames, then reconstructs a structured recipe — ingredients with quantities, steps, prep and cook time, and estimated nutrition — and saves it to your personal cookbook.

How it works

1. Share a Reel

Forward any recipe Reel or post to the bot's Instagram DM inbox, the same way you'd share it with a friend.

2. It reads everything it can

The caption is the backbone — in practice it is the whole story. Instagram does not make a shared Reel's video available to us, so the caption is what gets read. (Audio transcription and frame analysis are built and would be used if the media were ever obtainable.)

3. AI reconstructs the recipe

Ingredients, quantities, steps, timing, and nutrition are extracted and validated, then sent back to you as a saved recipe card.

4. It lands in your cookbook

Saved recipes build into a personal cookbook you can revisit, scale, and pull into a shopping list.

Every field says how confident it is

This is the part we care about most. An AI reconstructing a recipe from a 30-second video is going to be uncertain about some things — the bot never hides that. Every extracted field is tagged with how it was known, and the interface never presents an estimate with the same visual weight as something the creator actually said.

Stated by the creator Seen on screen Estimated by the model

Nutrition gets the same treatment, per nutrient rather than as one block — calories and macros, plus fibre, sugar, sodium and a range of vitamins and minerals where they can be estimated with reasonable grounding. If a creator says a dish is "540 calories, 48g protein" but doesn't mention carbs or fat, only calories and protein are labelled as creator-stated — everything else is calculated separately and labelled as such. When a creator has stated a figure, that is the number shown, and our own estimate is kept visible alongside it as a cross-check rather than silently replacing what they said.

Micronutrients deserve a specific caveat: creators almost never state them, so nearly all of them are model estimates rather than anything measured. They are labelled accordingly and shown with less weight than figures a creator actually gave. A nutrient the bot cannot estimate with reasonable grounding is left out entirely rather than guessed at to fill the panel.

Built on Meta's official messaging API

Recipe Reel Bot talks to Instagram exclusively through Meta's official Instagram Messaging API. It does not scrape Instagram, and it only ever sees content a user actively shares with it via DM.

Every recipe credits its creator

Recipes are derived from other people's posts, so the original creator's username and a link back to their post are shown alongside every saved recipe. The bot never redistributes or rehosts the original video — it only reads it, once, to build the recipe.

Please verify before you cook

Extracted recipes and nutrition figures are automated estimates, not dietary, nutritional, or medical advice. Always double-check anything that matters to your health or safety — allergens especially — against the original post or your own judgment.
