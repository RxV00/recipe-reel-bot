Terms of Service

Recipe Reel Bot is an early-access, personal project, not a commercial service. By messaging the bot on Instagram, you agree to the terms below.

What the service does

You share an Instagram Reel or post with the bot's Instagram DM inbox. The bot reads the post's caption and, where available, its audio track and sampled video frames, and uses AI to reconstruct a structured recipe, which is saved to your personal cookbook. The bot communicates with Instagram exclusively through Meta's official Instagram Messaging API and does not scrape Instagram.

Accuracy disclaimer — please read

Extracted recipes and nutrition figures are automated estimates produced by AI models, not dietary, nutritional, or medical advice. They may be wrong, incomplete, or mislabelled despite the confidence system described on the homepage. Always verify anything that matters before you rely on it — allergens, cook temperatures, and nutrition figures especially — against the original post or another trusted source.
